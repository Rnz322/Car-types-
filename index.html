Essential Driving Safety Tips

1. Always Wear Your Seatbelt
It's the simplest and most effective way to save lives in an accident.
2. Avoid Distractions
Stay focused—don’t text, eat, or use your phone while driving.
3. Follow Speed Limits
Speeding increases the risk of accidents and makes them more severe.
4. Use Turn Signals
Let others know your intentions to reduce confusion and prevent collisions.
5. Never Drive Under the Influence
Alcohol and drugs severely impair your judgment and reaction time.
6. Adjust for Weather Conditions
Drive slower and increase your following distance in rain or fog.
7. Stay Alert & Rested
Fatigue can be just as dangerous as being drunk behind the wheel.
